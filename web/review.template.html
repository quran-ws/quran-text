Overview

What the alignment found

Two words in five are spelled identically everywhere. Most of the rest differ only in vowelling. The part that needs a scholar's eye is the last 1.3 %: 985 words where the riwāyāt disagree about letters, word boundaries, or whether the word is there at all.

RiwāyahالروايةQāriʾCounting ĀyātWordsSource document

The āyah totals differ because the riwāyāt follow different counting traditions — Kūfī 6,236, Madanī 6,214, Baṣrī 6,217, and the Makkī count KFGQPC uses for Bazzī, 6,220. That is precisely why the index is flat: nesting words under āyāt would make one ID point at a different word in each riwāyah.

Source issues

Defects found in the KFGQPC packages

Six riwāyāt ship two releases. Comparing a document against its own publisher's other release is the sharpest check available, and it found three defects.

RiwāyahĀyāt comparedIdentical Notation onlyMarks / vowels Rasm differs

The marks / vowels column is dominated by the 2026 files adopting the Arabic Extended-B alif letters (U+0870–U+0879), which fold an alif and its vowel into one codepoint where the 2022 files used an alif plus combining marks. The rasm is untouched, so none of it reaches the word index.
